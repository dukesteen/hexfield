## P2P

Folded in by reference from [`docs/verification/knights-p2p/acceptance.md`](../knights-p2p/acceptance.md) (design: DECISIONS "Knights progress cards over the verified P2P protocol", 2026-09-29):

- a certified four-seat knights game through the real in-memory network with every one of the 23 hand-held progress cards played, audited `ok` and `complete` (`packages/protocol/src/knights-progress-game.test.ts`);
- the cheater suite on that game's certified entries, each forgery refused on its move with the signer as the certified offender;
- the nine chaos scenarios on a **verified** four-seat knights game ([`chaos-4p/`](../knights-p2p/chaos-4p/)), all 1/1 with complete audits (three peers audit in scenario 6, which excludes a Byzantine proposer).

### Known P2P limitations

- **Wedding, Master Merchant and Commercial Harbor move public kinds in verified games.** Their engine `'hidden'` variants are refused by the proof plan (`hand-steal-unavailable`), so other seats see which kinds were given, taken or swapped, as with a trade (not the hands). A hidden multi-card transfer would need the steal's receipt-and-dispute phase for the recipient's blindings.
- **A bad Master Merchant sealed hand stalls instead of producing a public cheat record.** The target's sealed hand opening is checked only by the merchant, who cannot build the debit proofs and stalls; there is no dispute record.
- The Commercial Harbor debit pre-proof is unit-tested and used whenever public bounds do not vouch for the offered card, but no test forces that case end to end.

## Not met, and caveats

- The simulation ran 1,000 games per scenario and seat count, not 20,000 (3–4 seats) and 10,000 (5–6 seats), at the user's direction.
- No automated browser test plays a whole knights game to a result on a phone viewport (dropped at the user's direction); the mobile criterion rests on the phone-viewport test above, its screenshots, and the user's own play on the live site. No physical-device check.
- No full `pnpm test` run exited 0 on the shared, heavily loaded machine. The first run passed every test and was marked failed only by vitest RPC timeouts; the second had load-induced timeouts (see [Final checks](#final-checks)).
- The P2P limitations above: public kinds for Wedding, Master Merchant and Commercial Harbor in verified games, and a stall rather than a public cheat record for a bad Master Merchant seal.
