## Remaining acceptance order

All explicit Stage 10 acceptance criteria and the bounded transfer-browser
checks in this map now have current-v6 evidence. Earlier failures remain
archived as diagnostics and do not override the passing bounded repeats.

The three-second target, five chaos additions, per-sequence private comparison,
takeover/return, native deletion/history, snapshot and unlock interruption
checks have current-v6 evidence linked above. A trace-specific limit is not an
additional acceptance requirement unless it maps to an explicit Stage 10 or
bounded-policy criterion.
