## Remaining acceptance order

500-seed repetition counts. It retains all five chaos additions, exact
private-state comparison at every certified sequence, each signing/storage
interruption boundary, and the original performance and browser requirements.
None of those gates is complete merely because the count changed.

1. Check peer-ready refresh timing after the current performance changes; one full repeat passed three seconds, while the earlier samples narrowly missed it.
2. Run the named storage/signing interruption cases and compare reconstructed private state at every sequence of the representative lifecycle fixture.
3. Complete the separate mixed-engine browser matrix and the remaining native deletion/retention checks.

The current-v6 encrypted restart, finish, audit, history and hundredth-commit snapshot checks have passed. Separate installed-Chrome traces now also pass the four-human lifecycle through two takeovers, fresh-key return, default-10-VP recovered-bot finish and three independent clean audits, plus certified cancellation with continued source play and cancelled destination reload. These named traces do not establish the separate mixed-engine/device matrix, every-sequence private-state comparison or unmeasured mobile/network performance.
