# Transfer storage review disposition

The review input is `transfer-storage-review-raw.md`. These changes address its four findings; they do not change certified transfer authorization or permit staged material to vote.

1. **Repeated promotion and safety reset:** promotion now writes a durable authorization tombstone in the same IndexedDB transaction as the new binding, certified prefix, and fresh safety. Stage and readiness insertions check that tombstone in their own readwrite transactions. Promotion checks it again. A retransmitted import cannot restage or regenerate fresh safety after activation, including after selective deletion of the active journal and binding. The tombstone is in the same database; deleting the _entire_ database deletes it too. This is not a cross-origin or cross-profile safety record.
2. **Unpromoted private imports:** promotion deletes every staged parent and readiness slot for its authorization in that transaction. `TransferImportStore.cancelCertified` independently replays a complete certified prefix and requires that exact authorization's cancelled outcome before it writes a cancellation tombstone and deletes those slots atomically. An arbitrary caller flag cannot erase them. An authorization that never reaches activation or certified cancellation remains staged; its durable custody and explicit user cleanup policy remain integration concerns.
3. **Secret copies:** the byte store wipes fetched and write copies after detaching its returned value or finishing a transaction. Journal load, safety, initialize, and commit paths wipe fetched binding copies. Promotion also wipes fetched stage/readiness copies on failure, encoded binding writes, and decoded staged private arrays, including the sealed package. These wipes reduce avoidable application heap copies; IndexedDB/browser internals are outside this guarantee.
4. **Bounds:** stage input gets a bounded structural preflight before entry hashing, schema parsing, or canonical encoding, using the byte store's configured limit. Promotion applies the journal's configured per-record bound to genesis and every certified entry it writes. Narrow-limit regressions confirm failure leaves the destination journal absent.

Focused fake-IndexedDB tests cover certified activation, restaging after promotion, selective journal/binding deletion with surviving tombstone, cancellation, missing cancellation refusal, transaction rollback, and two-tab promotion races. The protocol participant must still authenticate and reconstruct sealed private material before staging; opaque package bytes alone do not authorize import.
